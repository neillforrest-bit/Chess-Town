# Grammar content review

128 original multiple-choice questions, 32 per exact difficulty. English grammar is a new negative-ID house category (-14), using the same focused-topic path as NBA. No external question generation or model calls.

- Easy: parts of speech, articles, basic agreement, plurals and simple tenses.
- Medium: everyday usage, perfect forms, possessives, relative pronouns, tags and conditionals.
- Hard: complex clauses, formal case, mandative subjunctive, verb complements, parallelism and modifiers.
- Master: inversion, clefts, clause functions, ellipsis, modal inference and complex verb patterns.
- Classic retains the core ladder: easy/easy/medium/medium/hard. Fixed levels remain fixed.

Questions assume standard written English. Formal or subjunctive constructions are labelled in the prompt where the distinction affects the key. Avoided dialect-dependent collective-noun agreement, Oxford-comma style choices, and disputed bans on sentence-final prepositions or split infinitives. Stop/regret items specify intended meaning so both constructions cannot be defended under different readings. Each prompt has exactly four distinct answer strings. The bank is finite: no-repeat tracking cycles after a shelf is used; unlimited fresh questions are not promised.
